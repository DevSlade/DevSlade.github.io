About the studio

Capybara Cartel is a one-person game studio making bright, honest games with some country in them.

The story so far

The story so far

Capybara Cartel started the way most things in the hills do: one person, a head full of ideas and more stubbornness than sense. It's a one-person studio in Tennessee, and Holler Pop was designed, built and play-tested at one desk, a level at a time.

The games are about home. Hollers and creeks, county fairs and sawmills, rickhouses full of barrels, and the stubborn old-timers who keep it all running. Bright and silly on the surface, made with real care underneath.

The plan is simple. Make games people actually enjoy. Charge fairly for the extras and never trick anybody into paying. Keep your data on your phone where it belongs. And answer every email.

Why a capybara?

The calmest animal alive

Capybaras get along with everybody. Birds stand on them, ducklings ride on them, and they just keep chewing. Nothing rattles them. That's the kind of studio this is meant to be: steady, friendly, and in no hurry to cut corners.

The "cartel" part is just for fun. The only thing being smuggled here is a little country onto your phone.
