How it plays

Match, roll and blast

Swap two bottles to line up three of a flavor and they pop. Make bigger shapes and things get loud.

Line up 4

Line clear

Four in a row clears the whole row. Four up and down takes the column.

L or T shape

Still Blast

Match in an L or a T and the still blows, taking out everything around it.

Line up 5

Golden Barrel

Swap it with any bottle and every bottle of that flavor leaves the board.

Lucky drop

Double Shot

Pop the bottle wearing the 2X badge for 20 seconds of double points.

Lucky drop

Lucky Coin

Pop it for a handful of coins. Every so often it hits the jackpot.

HOEDOWN!

Win with 5 or more moves to spare and the whole holler kicks off a Hoedown. Then it's your call: keep playing for bonus points, or finish the level and cash in what's left.

Your host

Meet Ol' Pete

Well howdy! Name's Pete. This holler used to make the finest mash in Copperhead County.

Pete's got a still to fix, crates in the road and mud on every porch, and he can't do it alone. He'll show you the ropes on the first few levels, then cheer you on all the way up the mountain.

The road up

100 levels, ten chapters

One county road, from the bottom of the holler to the top of the mountain. Each chapter is ten levels, and every level was play-tested and gets a little tougher than the last. Earn up to three stars on each one, then come back to beat your best score.

Trouble in the holler

Clear the mess

The holler's in a state. Each chapter brings new trouble to clear on the way to your goals.

  • Cobwebs

    Pop the bottle under a web to sweep it away.

  • Crates

    Matches next to a crate knock it apart. Some take two or three hits.

  • Frost

    A frozen bottle won't budge. Match it or blast it to crack the ice.

  • Kegs

    Clear the way under a keg and roll it down to the cellar.

  • Mud

    Mud spreads every turn you don't clear some. Match next to it to wash it off.

Play your way

No funny business

Holler Pop is free, and it plays fair.

  • No timersTake as long as you like on every move.
  • No ads in the middle of a levelAds never pop up on their own. A video only plays when you tap a button asking for one.
  • Videos when you want themWatch a short video for coins, a life, five more moves or a booster, as many times as you like.

Questions

Folks ask

When does it come out?

Soon. The game is finished and going through Google Play's testing now. Email us and we'll tell you the day it's out.

How much does it cost?

It's free to download and free to play all the way through. There are optional in-app purchases (coin packs and a few one-time extras) and optional reward videos.

Which phones can play it?

Android phones, through Google Play. There's no iPhone version yet.

Do I need the internet?

No. Holler Pop plays offline. You only need a connection to buy something or to watch an optional video.

Who is it for?

Players 13 and up. It's a cartoon game, but the bottles are whiskey flavors and Pete runs a still.

Is there real alcohol or real brands in it?

No. Every bottle, barrel and still in the game is made up, and no real whiskey brand or distillery appears in it. The game doesn't sell or promote alcohol.

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone. There's no account, so uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes your progress. One-time purchases come back with RESTORE PURCHASES in the store.

Something's broken. Who do I tell?

The support page has answers to the usual problems, or email support@capybaracartel.com.
