The short version

  • There's no account and nothing to sign up for. Your progress is saved on your phone, and we don't have a copy.
  • Capybara Cartel doesn't collect personal information from the game, and we don't sell personal information.
  • The optional reward videos come from Google AdMob. Google collects some device information to show them. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your answer later.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.
  • If you email us, we use what you send only to answer you.

1.Who we are

Holler Pop is a game made by Capybara Cartel, an independent game studio in Tennessee, USA ("Capybara Cartel", "we", "us"). This policy covers the Holler Pop app for Android and this website, capybaracartel.com.

We're responsible for the small amount of personal information we handle ourselves, which in practice means emails you send us. Google is responsible for the information it collects through its ad and payment services, under Google's own privacy policy.

By using Holler Pop or this website you agree to this policy and to our Terms of service. If you don't agree, please don't use them. Questions are always welcome at support@capybaracartel.com.

3.What stays on your phone

Holler Pop saves your game in the app's private storage on your phone. The save holds the levels you've unlocked, your stars and best scores, coins, boosters, lives and when the next life arrives, your sound, music and vibration settings, which of Pete's tips you've seen, the daily gift and video counters, and which one-time purchases you own, along with the store's confirmation IDs so a purchase is never paid out twice. Google's consent tool also stores your ad privacy choice on your phone.

None of this is sent to us. There's no account, no cloud save and no Capybara Cartel server, which also means we can't recover lost progress for you.

If backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include the game's save in your own Google account backup and put it back on a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account, and we can't see it.

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage (Settings → Apps → Holler Pop → Storage → Clear storage), deletes the save. The game uses the internet only for ads and purchases. It doesn't ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

4.Reward videos (Google AdMob)

Holler Pop offers optional reward videos: watch a short ad to get coins, a life, five more moves, a booster and similar rewards. A video plays only when you tap a button asking for one. There are no banner ads, and no ads pop up on their own before, during or after a level.

The videos come from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. So that a video is ready when you ask for one, the game loads ads in the background while it's open, after asking for consent where that's required. That means Google's ad software in the game can collect information even if you never watch a video, including:

  • your phone's advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address, which can show your approximate location;
  • device and app details, such as phone model, operating system, language, screen size and game version;
  • how you interact with ads, such as whether you watched a video to the end or tapped it;
  • diagnostics, such as crash logs and performance data.

Google uses this to show and measure ads, limit how often you see the same one, detect fraud and abuse, and, where you've allowed it, show ads based on your interests ("personalized ads"). Google may share it with the advertisers and ad networks that compete to show an ad. You can read more in Google's privacy policy, how Google uses information from apps that use its services, and Google's list of what its ad software collects.

Capybara Cartel doesn't receive your advertising ID or any of this information about you. AdMob gives us totals only, such as how many videos were shown and what they earned.

5.Your ad choices

  • Consent. If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, or anywhere else the law requires it, the game shows Google's consent message before it requests any ads. You can say no. You may then see fewer videos, or videos that aren't personalized. Everything else in the game works the same.
  • PRIVACY CHOICES. When choices apply to you, a PRIVACY CHOICES button appears in the game's Settings and in the store. Tap it to review or change your answer at any time.
  • Your advertising ID. You can reset or delete it in your phone's settings, usually Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads on some phones. Once it's deleted, apps can't use it to personalize ads.
  • Google's ad settings. You can turn off personalized ads from Google in My Ad Center.

6.Purchases (Google Play)

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. We never see your name, card number or billing address. When you buy something, Google Play sends the game a confirmation (an order ID, the item and the time). The game uses it to deliver the item and, for one-time purchases, to restore it with RESTORE PURCHASES. Google Play may share order details, such as the order ID and item, with us in our developer account for refunds and record keeping.

Refunds and billing questions are covered in the Terms of service.

7.The Unity engine

Holler Pop is built with the Unity engine from Unity Technologies, and uses Unity's in-app purchasing plugin to talk to Google Play. We've turned off Unity Analytics, Unity's crash reporting and its performance reporting, so the game doesn't send your gameplay to Unity. Unity's plugins may send Unity limited technical information about how they're running, such as plugin versions and your phone's operating system, under Unity's privacy policy.

8.When you email us

If you write to us, we get your email address, your name if your email shows it, and whatever you include, such as screenshots or your phone model. We use it only to answer you and to fix problems you report. We won't add you to a mailing list or send you marketing. If you ask us to tell you when Holler Pop comes out, we'll send that one email and nothing more.

Emails are stored with our email provider. You can ask us to delete yours at any time.

9.This website

capybaracartel.com is a plain website hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no sign-up, no analytics and no ad trackers, and it doesn't set cookies. When you visit:

  • GitHub, which hosts the site, receives your IP address and browser details so it can deliver the pages and keep its service secure. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Most pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when your browser fetches them. See Google Fonts and privacy.
  • The "Email us" buttons only open your own email app. Nothing is sent until you press send.

Because the site doesn't track you, browser signals such as Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control don't change anything here.

10.Children

Holler Pop is meant for players 13 and older and isn't directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The game has no chat and no way to post or share anything. If you're a parent and believe your child under 13 has sent us personal information, for example in an email, contact us and we'll delete it.

Parents can control purchases with Google Play's parental controls and purchase approvals, including Google Family Link.

11.How long it's kept

  • Your save: on your phone until you clear the game's storage or uninstall it.
  • Emails to us: until your question is settled and for a reasonable time after in case you write back, unless you ask us to delete them sooner or we need to keep them for a legal reason, such as a payment dispute.
  • Information held by Google or GitHub: for as long as their own policies say.

12.Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what personal information we have about you, ask for a copy, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it. Email support@capybaracartel.com. We'll answer within 30 days, or sooner if the law requires. We may need to confirm the request came from you. We won't treat you differently for using any of these rights.

Information that exists only on your phone is already under your control: clear the game's storage or uninstall it. We can't reach information held by Google. Use your Google account's data and privacy settings and the choices in section 5.

If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland

We rely on these legal bases:

  • Consent for personalized ads, and for storing or reading information on your device for ads. Google's consent message asks for this, and you can withdraw it at any time with PRIVACY CHOICES.
  • Legitimate interests for non-personalized ads, ad measurement and fraud prevention, for answering your emails, and for keeping this website running and secure.
  • Performing our contract with you for processing and delivering purchases.

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the use of your personal information, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw consent. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

If you live in a US state with a privacy law

Residents of states such as California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas and Oregon may have the right to know, access, correct and delete personal information, and to opt out of its sale, its "sharing", or its use for targeted advertising. We don't sell personal information for money. Some of these laws may count personalized ads shown through Google as "sharing" or "targeted advertising". To opt out, use PRIVACY CHOICES in the game when it appears, delete or reset your advertising ID (section 5), or email us. If we turn down a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer with "Appeal" in the subject line.

13.Security

Your save lives in the game's private storage, which Android keeps apart from other apps. The game's connections to Google for ads and purchases are encrypted. We take reasonable steps to protect the little information we hold, but no system is perfectly secure.

14.Other countries

We're based in the United States. Google, GitHub and our email provider may process information in the US and other countries, whose data protection laws may differ from yours. Where the law requires it, these companies use legal safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

15.Changes to this policy

We'll update this policy when the game or the law changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change is significant, we'll also point it out on this site or in the game. Continuing to use Holler Pop after a change means you accept the updated policy.
