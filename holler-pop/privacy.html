Holler Pop Privacy Policy

Capybara Cartel · Tennessee · Last updated November 3, 2026

Holler Pop is a match-3 puzzle game made by Capybara Cartel. This page explains what information the game handles and why. The short version: we don't collect your personal information. If the game shows optional video ads, the ad provider collects some device information to show them.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (levels, stars, lives, boosters, sound settings and whether you bought Remove Ads) is saved only on your device. Capybara Cartel doesn't receive it. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Optional video ads

Some versions of Holler Pop offer rewards, like extra moves or a life, if you choose to watch a short video. Ads never play unless you tap a button asking for one. These ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in your phone's settings;
  • approximate location worked out from your IP address;
  • how you interact with the ads, and diagnostic information such as crash logs.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before any ad loads, and you can change your choice later in Settings. You can read how Google uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Purchases such as Remove Ads are handled by Google Play. Capybara Cartel never sees your payment details. The game receives only a confirmation that the item was bought, so it can restore it if you reinstall.

Children

Holler Pop is meant for players aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Delete everything the game stored: uninstall it, or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Holler Pop.
  • Ask us anything, or ask us to delete data: email us at the address below.
