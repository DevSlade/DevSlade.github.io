How things run here

House rules

Four promises for every Capybara Cartel game. If one of our games ever breaks one, email us and say so.

  1. No loot boxes.Nothing you pay for is a gamble. You always know exactly what you're getting.
  2. No ads in the middle of play.A video only plays when you tap a button asking for one, and it pays you for watching.
  3. Winnable for free.Every level can be beaten without spending a cent. Paying is a thank-you, never a toll.
  4. No sign-up, no servers.Your progress lives on your phone, not ours. The ad network's part is spelled out in the privacy policy.

Who's behind the cap

One developer, one capybara

Capybara Cartel is one developer in Tennessee, making games about home: hollers, stills, county fairs and the stubborn folks who keep them going.

The capybara is the calmest animal alive. That's the plan here. No crunch, no tricks, and games made to be played, not milked.
