About

Who's behind the cap

Capybara Cartel is one developer in Tennessee, making games about home: hollers, stills, county fairs and the stubborn folks who keep them going.

The capybara is the calmest animal alive. That's the plan here. No crunch, no tricks, and games that are made to be played, not milked.

House rules

  1. No loot boxes. Not now, not later.
  2. No ads in the middle of play. An ad only shows when you tap a button asking for one.
  3. Fully winnable for free. Paying is a thank-you, never a toll.
  4. We don't collect your personal info. Your progress saves on your phone, not our servers.
