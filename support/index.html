Progress and saving

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone, in the game's own storage. There's no account and no cloud save, so we don't have a copy.

I got a new phone. Can I bring my progress?

Only through Android's own backup. If backup was on for your old phone and you restore from it while setting up the new one, Android may bring your Holler Pop save along. Otherwise progress can't be moved. One-time upgrades you bought come back with RESTORE PURCHASES.

I lost my progress. Can you get it back?

We're sorry, but no. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes the save, and since nothing is kept on a server, there's no copy to restore. If you bought Remove Ads, Big Jar or Quick Refill, use RESTORE PURCHASES in the store to get them back.

Purchases and restoring

I paid, but my item didn't show up

Make sure you're online, then close the game fully and open it again. The game checks with Google Play when it starts. Still missing? Open the store and tap RESTORE PURCHASES. If that doesn't do it, email us with your Google Play order number and we'll sort it out.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the store in the game and tap RESTORE PURCHASES while signed in to the same Google account you bought with. That brings back one-time upgrades: Remove Ads, Big Jar and Quick Refill. Coins, boosters and lives get used up, so they can't be restored.

How do I get a refund?

Google Play handles payments, so the quickest way is Google Play's refund request page. You can also email us with your order number and we'll take a look. Refunded purchases are removed from the game. The details are in the Terms of service.

Can I stop my kids from buying things?

Yes. In the Google Play app, go to your profile picture → Settings → Authentication → Require authentication for purchases, and pick "For all purchases". Google Family Link can also require a parent's approval.

Videos and Remove Ads

Will ads interrupt my game?

No. Holler Pop has no banner ads and no ads that pop up on their own. A video only plays when you tap a button asking for one, and you get a reward for watching it.

What does Remove Ads do?

It lets you skip the video and collect the reward straight away, up to 20 times a day. After that, rewards need a video again until the next day.

The video button isn't working

Videos need an internet connection, and sometimes there just isn't an ad ready. Wait a minute and try again. If you said no to ads in the privacy message, you may see fewer videos. You can change that with PRIVACY CHOICES in Settings.

I watched a video but didn't get my reward

The reward only comes if the video plays to the end. If you did watch it all and nothing arrived, email us and tell us which reward it was.

Lives, coins and boosters

How do lives work?

You have up to 5 lives (8 with Big Jar). You lose one when you run out of moves, quit a level from the pause menu, or close the game in the middle of a level. Win with a Hoedown and you can leave without losing anything. Lives come back one at a time, every 30 minutes, or every 15 with Quick Refill. You can also watch a video for a life.

I'm stuck on a level

Try a booster: the Mallet smashes any one bottle or blocker, Swap Hands swaps any two bottles, and the Lantern shows you a good move. When you're out of moves, you can watch a video for 5 more. Every level can be beaten without paying.

How do I get more coins and boosters?

Win levels, take the daily gift, fill the coin bag by watching videos, or watch a video for a booster. Coin packs are in the store if you'd like to support the game.

Privacy and your data

Does Holler Pop collect my information?

We don't. Your progress stays on your phone. Google's ad software collects some device information to show the reward videos. The Privacy policy spells out exactly what.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

If choices apply where you live, tap PRIVACY CHOICES in the game's Settings or in the store. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, usually Settings → Privacy → Ads.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstall the game, or go to Settings → Apps → Holler Pop → Storage → Clear storage. That deletes everything the game saved. To delete emails you've sent us, ask us. For information held by Google, use your Google account's privacy settings.

For parents

Is Holler Pop OK for my kid?

It's made for players 13 and up. It's a bright cartoon puzzle game with no chat, no strangers and no way to share anything. The bottles are whiskey flavors and Pete runs a still, all made up and played for laughs. No real alcohol brand is in it, and the game doesn't promote drinking.

It has optional purchases and optional reward videos. Turning on purchase approval in Google Play (see "Can I stop my kids from buying things?") keeps spending in your hands.
